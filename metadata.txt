    原版が採用する「林業の収益性」と「山地災害リスク」の両面から森林管理の方向性を検討する考え方と、基本的な処理体系を継承しています。
    謝辞：原版は、林野庁の委託事業において、日本森林技術協会がゾーニングの考え方と作業フローを取りまとめ、MIERUNE Inc.がQGISプラグインとして実装したものです。
    MORIZON NEXTは日本森林技術協会または林野庁による公式版ではありません。

    MORIZON NEXT is an independent, unofficial continuation of "MORIZON" (もりぞん), a forest zoning support tool originally developed through a project commissioned by the Forestry Agency of Japan.
    It inherits the original's approach of examining forest management direction from two perspectives, forestry profitability and mountain disaster risk, along with its core processing framework.
    Acknowledgements: The original MORIZON was developed through a project commissioned by the Forestry Agency of Japan, with the zoning workflow organized by the Japan Forest Technology Association and the QGIS plugin implemented by MIERUNE Inc.
    MORIZON NEXT is not an official release of the Japan Forest Technology Association or the Forestry Agency of Japan.
